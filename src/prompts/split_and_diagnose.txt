SCOPES for the same part — e.g. a minor repair/reseal vs a full component REPLACEMENT — AND that
scope difference is visible in BOTH a bimodal cost spread AND a meaningful labor-hours difference.

one repair with normal price variation, not two repairs). Do not split on trivial wording differences.
